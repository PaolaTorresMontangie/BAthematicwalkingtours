Masonic Walking Tour in Buenos Aires

★ 4.9 (see reviews)

What will we do on this tour?

From the National Congress to Café Tortoni and Avenida de Mayo, discover how Buenos Aires was built upon Masonic principles of enlightenment, reason, and symmetry. Learn to read the city as a temple of light and geometry. Every stone, every façade, every angle hides a story. Explore the invisible network that connects the most mysterious places in Buenos Aires.

Tour details

🕰 Duration: 2 hours

🎧 Language: English

💵 Private tours or small group tours available

Tour itinerary

📍 Recoleta Cemetery - Where Masonic symbols rest in silence

📍 Masonic Lodge on Perón Street – The Masonic heart of the city

📍 National Congress – Geometry of power and light

📍 Café Tortoni – Meeting place for poets and initiates

📍 Confitería del Molino – An Art Nouveau temple of perfection

📍 Casa Rosada – Power wrapped in symbolism

📍 Palacio Barolo – Is Dante’s Divine Comedy hidden inside a building?
