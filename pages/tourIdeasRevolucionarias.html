Revolutionary Ideas Tour

Ideas that changed a Nation

What will we explore on this tour?

Not all Masonic power was carved in stone. Some of it was forged through ideas, education, and words. This tour reveals how Freemasonry influenced classrooms, cafés, and the debates that quietly shaped Buenos Aires and modern Argentina.

Tour details

🕰 Duration: 2 hours

🎧 Language: English

💵 Private tours or small groups available

Tour itinerary

📍 Plaza de Mayo - Birthplace of the Revolution (1810)

📍 Manzana de las Luces - Knowledge as power

📍 Colegio Nacional de Buenos Aires - Education as a revolutionary weapon

📍 Former Newspaper District (La Prensa / La Nación area) - Words that shaped the nation

📍 Café Tortoni - Cafés as unofficial lodges

📍 Congreso de la Nación - From ideas to institutions

📍 Avenida de Mayo at sunset
