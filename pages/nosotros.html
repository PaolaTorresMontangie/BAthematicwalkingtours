About BA Thematic Walking Tours

Maria Luz La Rocca - Local Expert Guide

Luz is an expert in Argentine tourism and an English-speaking guide, passionate about revealing the hidden. She studied Tourism in Buenos Aires and worked as a guide at the Teatro Colón in the same city. Later, during the six years she lived in London, she became fascinated by the architecture and culture of the various European cities she visited and returned with a desire to re-evaluate her own culture through visits to foreigners. Her fascination with Freemasonry began while observing architecture in Europe and Buenos Aires, where geometry, symbolism, and power converge.

With years of experience guiding international visitors, and visiting all kind of Cultural points in many European cities, she transforms each walk into an intimate exploration of the city’s hidden soul. Luz believes every monument, every symbol, and every story carries a message — if you know how to look.

“My tours are not about what you see — they’re about what you discover”
