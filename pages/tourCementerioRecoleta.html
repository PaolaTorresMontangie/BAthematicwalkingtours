Recoleta Cemetery Masonic Tour

Uncover the intriguing secrets and forgotten tales of the city

What will we do on this tour?

Walk among marble angels, cryptic symbols and the stories of Argentina’s most powerful families. Discover the hidden Masonic ideals embedded in Recoleta’s architecture and the spiritual codes behind its beauty.

Tour details

🕰 Duration: 1.5 hours

🎧 Language: English

💵 Private and small-group tours available

Tour itinerary

📍 Main Entrance - Notice neoclassical gate & columns

📍 William Brown - Irish immigrant → Father of Argentine Navy

📍 Presidential section: Mitre- Avellaneda-Sarmiento (Father of Education)– Roca

📍 Boxer, “Wild Bull of the Pampas

📍 Nobel Prize in Chemistry, Federico Leloir

📍 Liliana Crociatti: Young bride, tragedy, dog statue

📍 Rufina Cambaceres: 19-year-old socialite, buried alive legend

📍 Evita Peron: Adored by poor, hated by elite
